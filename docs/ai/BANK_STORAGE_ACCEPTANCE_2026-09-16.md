## Accepted behavior

- Account-wide Bank stored outside individual character inventory.
- Initial capacity is 40 slots.
- Stackable items use one slot per stack.
- Partial stack deposits and withdrawals work.
- Bound/non-tradable items are rejected.
- Equipped-only copies are protected; spare copies remain transferable.
- Bank mutations are server-authoritative and atomic.
- Bank access is Base-only and physically proximity-gated.
- Bank UI exposes Inventory and Bank panes with authoritative capacity state.
- Moving out of Bank range closes the UI.
- Bank contents remain authoritative when the panel is reopened.
- Existing DungeonMMO runtime behavior showed no new red runtime errors during the interaction proof.
